About RandA Tours

A local team built around your story.

RandA Tours was founded to provide safe, in-depth information and wonderful explanations of Sri Lanka, together with opportunities to explore its mystery and history.

We specialize in tours and treks across Sri Lanka, supported by professional staff, tour guides, trekking guides and Buddhist professionals with years of tourism experience.

Alongside classic holidays, we arrange active experiences such as rafting, mountain biking and wildlife safaris. Our approach is simple: every program is checked, double-checked and shaped around the people who travel with us.
